Hints

Mouse and keyboard

  • Drag across cells, or Shift+click, to select several cells. Ctrl+click (or drag) deselects.
  • Z X C V choose Digit, Candidate, Pairs or Colour; Space cycles through them
  • Hold Shift while typing or clicking a number to enter candidates
  • In Colour mode, 1–9 paint a colour; 0 or Backspace clears it
  • Arrow keys move, Shift+arrow extends the selection, Backspace erases
  • H hint, Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Y undo and redo, Esc closes the hint
