Hints

Mouse and keyboard

  • Drag across cells, or Shift+click, to select several cells. Ctrl+click (or drag) deselects.
  • Z X C V choose Digit, Candidate, Pairs or Colour; Space cycles through them
  • Hold Shift while typing or clicking a number to enter candidates
  • In Colour mode, 1–9 paint a colour; 0 or Backspace clears it. There are three palettes (solid, stripes / and stripes \) of the same nine colours: the Palette button switches between them, the eye button hides or shows the current one.
  • Arrow keys move, Shift+arrow extends the selection, Backspace erases
  • H hint, Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Y undo and redo, Esc closes the hint
  • On a phone, hold the S key like a Shift key: while holding it, tap cells to add them to the selection, and digits go in as candidates. In Colour mode, tap Clear colour to clear the selected cells, or hold it (the label turns into “Clear all colour”) to clear every colour in all palettes. Swiping up or down over the buttons scrolls the page.
  • The timer pauses while the page is hidden and after 2 minutes without input.

Privacy

SudokuG has no accounts, no cookies and no tracking. Your settings, games, stats, liked puzzles and the list of puzzles you've played are stored only in this browser and never sent anywhere. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages; like any web host, GitHub sees your IP address when the page loads (GitHub's privacy statement).
